What is CalcUni?

CalcUni is a premium, comprehensive online calculator platform designed to provide quick, accurate, and free mathematical answers to users worldwide. Developed by Fiable Technologies in Canada, the platform serves as a trusted utility for diverse calculation needs across professional, educational, and domestic contexts.

Core Calculation Categories Served

  • Finance & Retirement: Estimate mortgage payments, compound interest growth, personal loans, car loan repayments, and retirement planning.
  • Taxation: Compute federal and provincial income taxes, sales taxes (GST/HST/PST), Value Added Tax (VAT), and payroll deductions.
  • Construction & Material Estimation: Calculate concrete volume, square footage, paint coverage, tile requirements, and roofing shingles.
  • Health & Fitness: Estimate Body Mass Index (BMI), Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR), target weight ranges, and daily calorie needs.
  • Math & Education: Access percentage calculations, fraction arithmetic, scientific calculators, average sets, course grades, and weighted GPA.
  • Technology & Everyday Utilities: Find IP subnets, CIDR masks, network bandwidth speeds, digital storage conversion, secure password generation, and tip splits.
