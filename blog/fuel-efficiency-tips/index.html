Ultimate Guide to Fuel Efficiency & Gas Mileage Optimization

Increase your car's MPG and reduce gas spending. Learn how simple tire adjustments, aerodynamic upgrades, and shifting gears save fuel costs.

Written by the CalcUni Editorial Team | Updated: May 2026

With fuel prices fluctuating constantly, optimizing your vehicle's fuel efficiency is one of the easiest ways to put money back in your pocket. Fuel economy is measured in Miles Per Gallon (MPG) in imperial systems, or Liters per 100 Kilometers (L/100km) in metric countries.

1. The Two Main Calculations

Miles Per Gallon (MPG)

Measures how many miles your car travels on one gallon of gasoline:

MPG = Distance Traveled (miles) / Fuel Added (gallons)

Liters per 100 Kilometers (L/100km)

Measures how many liters of fuel your car consumes to travel 100 kilometers (lower values are more efficient):

L/100km = [ Fuel Consumed (L) / Distance Traveled (km) ] × 100

2. Top 4 Tips to Boost Fuel Economy

  1. Maintain Tire Pressure: Under-inflated tires increase rolling resistance, making your engine work harder. Keeping tires inflated to the manufacturer-recommended PSI can improve gas mileage by up to 3%.
  2. Drive Smoothly: Aggressive acceleration and hard braking waste massive amounts of fuel. Gentle acceleration and coasting up to stoplights can improve highway mileage by 15% to 30%.
  3. Reduce Excess Weight: Every extra 100 pounds carried in your trunk reduces fuel economy by 1%. Clear out heavy gear, toolboxes, and roof racks when not in use.
  4. Observe Speed Limits: Aerodynamic drag increases exponentially at higher speeds. For most vehicles, fuel efficiency drops rapidly at speeds above 50 mph (80 km/h).
