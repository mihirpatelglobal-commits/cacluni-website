The Paint Calculator Formula

The calculation relies on the following standard formula:

Gallons Required = [ (Wall Area - Window/Door Area) / 350 ] * Coats

Where: * Wall Area = total wall length multiplied by height * 350 represents the average coverage in square feet per gallon of paint * Coats = number of paint layers planned (usually 2) Explanation: This formula estimates the quantity of paint needed to cover a room's wall surface, subtracting major openings like windows and doors, and factoring in multiple coats and primer requirements.

Step-by-Step Worked Example

Example Calculation

Inputs: * Room Perimeter = 48 feet (4 walls of 12 feet each) * Wall Height = 8 feet * Windows/Doors Area = 40 sq ft * Number of Coats = 2 Calculation: * Step 1: Calculate Gross Wall Area: 48 * 8 = 384 sq ft * Step 2: Calculate Net Wall Area: 384 - 40 = 344 sq ft * Step 3: Divide by coverage rate: 344 / 350 = 0.98 gallons per coat * Step 4: Multiply by coats: 0.98 * 2 = 1.96 gallons Result: * Required Paint = 2.0 Gallons What This Means: You should buy 2 gallons of paint to apply 2 full coats to the walls of the room.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

❓ How does wall surface texture affect paint coverage?

Textured walls (like stucco, popcorn, or brick) have significantly more surface area than smooth drywall. Textured surfaces can reduce paint coverage by 20% to 35%, meaning you will need to purchase more paint to cover the same footprint.

❓ When is a separate primer coat required and how does it affect paint volume?

A primer coat is essential when painting raw drywall, wood, or porous masonry, and when transitioning from a dark color to a light color. Primer seals the surface so it does not absorb your expensive paint, reducing the total paint coats needed from 3+ to 2.

❓ How do you calculate paint requirements for multiple coats?

Calculate the net surface area of the walls, divide by the coverage factor (typically 350 sq ft per gallon), and multiply by the number of coats. Most standard interior painting projects require 2 coats for an even finish and true color representation.

❓ How much paint wastage should I estimate for trim and ceilings?

Add a standard 10% wastage margin to cover spills, brush/roller absorption, and touch-ups. For trim and baseboards, calculate their linear footage separately and allocate approximately 1 gallon of trim paint per 400 linear feet.

❓ How do I store and dispose of leftover paint safely?

Store leftover paint in a sealed container in a cool, dry place. To dispose of latex paint, let it dry out completely by adding paint hardener or cat litter, then dispose of it with regular trash. Do not pour liquid paint down drains.
