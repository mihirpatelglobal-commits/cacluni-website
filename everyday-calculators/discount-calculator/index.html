The Discount Calculator Formula

The calculation relies on the following standard formula:

Discounted Price = Original Price * (1 - Discount Percentage)

Determines the final retail price of a product after subtracting a promotional discount rate from the original price tag.

Step-by-Step Worked Example

Example Calculation

A jacket originally costs $80, and is currently on sale for 25% off: Discounted Price = $80 * (1 - 0.25) = $80 * 0.75 = $60.00. You save exactly $20.
